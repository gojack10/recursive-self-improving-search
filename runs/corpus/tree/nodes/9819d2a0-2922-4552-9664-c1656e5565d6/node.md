**Kind:** What We Learned

The supplied table attributes 143.9 correct finds per dollar to Parallel, 96.3 to Brave and 82.6 to Exa. These are observational totals, not marginal unique-find yields or controlled accuracy comparisons. They support retaining Parallel as default while testing one cost-capped Brave discovery challenge, measuring new verified identities rather than URL novelty. Search-result overlap literature provides a general motivation (https://bendersky.github.io/pubs/2010-5.pdf), not evidence of a Telem-specific gain. No improvement has yet been measured.

**Tested scope:** Hypothesis: selective discovery-provider diversification under a fixed budget
