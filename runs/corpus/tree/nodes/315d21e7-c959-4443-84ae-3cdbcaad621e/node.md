**Kind:** What We Learned

Hypothesis, not a measured improvement: distinguish an unproductive cohort from a query that fails to express the source's terminology. Stanford's relevance-feedback guidance identifies synonymy as a recall problem (https://nlp.stanford.edu/IR-book/html/htmledition/relevance-feedback-and-query-expansion-1.html); systematic-search guidance recommends harvesting terminology and testing retrieval against known relevant records (https://library.cumc.columbia.edu/kb/developing-and-validating-comprehensive-literature). Adapt this with one bounded repair using terms actually observed in relevant evidence, not unlimited paraphrases. Retrieval of known positives diagnoses a route but cannot establish completeness or validate other candidates. No unseen-hunt benefit has yet been demonstrated.

**Tested scope:** Query formulation and interpretation of zero-progress cohort samples
